This is one of the most powerful aspects of modern face recognition models like FaceNet: we do not actually need to train the model on your students' images!

Here is how the data and matching work under the hood:

1. The Pre-trained "Brain" (FaceNet)
The FaceNet model (InceptionResnetV1(pretrained='vggface2')) we loaded is already pre-trained on a dataset containing millions of faces (the VGGFace2 dataset).

Because it has already seen millions of faces, it has learned how to extract general facial features (distance between eyes, nose shape, jawline, etc.).
It is capable of turning any human face into a list of 512 numbers (called a Face Embedding).
2. How Students are "Registered" (Zero-Shot Learning)
When you register a student (either via Webcam or by uploading a photo):

MTCNN detects the face in the image and crops it.
FaceNet reads that crop and generates the 512-dimensional embedding (a list of 512 floats).
We store this list of numbers directly in the SQLite database (StudentProfile.face_encoding).
No training is required! Once those 512 numbers are saved under that student's database row, the registration is complete. You don't even need to keep the original image file on the disk!
